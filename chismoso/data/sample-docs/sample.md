# AI Trends 2026

A short briefing on emerging AI trends shaping Latin-American startups
across Bogota, Medellin, Cali and the wider Andean corridor. This document
is intentionally over five hundred characters so the marked lexer exercises
its token-walking path instead of returning the raw markdown verbatim.

## Restaurant automation as the leading vertical

Restaurant automation is the fastest-growing vertical in Colombia for
2026. Cloud POS systems paired with WhatsApp Business APIs are cutting
order friction in tier-2 cities, and kitchen display software is helping
small eateries keep margins above water. Operators report that automating
the reservation and order-taking flow frees staff for hospitality work and
reduces no-shows, which is the single largest source of revenue leakage
for independent restaurants in the region.

## Tooling landscape

The 2026 tooling landscape is consolidating around three pillars:

- Vector databases with sub-50ms recall at the edge
- Multi-agent orchestration frameworks that compile to deterministic graphs
- Cheap inference via small open models running on consumer GPUs

## WhatsApp as a default channel

WhatsApp remains the default channel for reservations, payment
confirmations, and delivery coordination across Latin America. Founders
who treat WhatsApp as a first-class integration surface rather than a
secondary notification pipe consistently outperform peers who bolt it on
as an afterthought.
